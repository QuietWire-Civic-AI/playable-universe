# Tile Protocol v0

A **tile** is the first small, composable unit of the Playable Universe interface.

A tile may represent:
- an attested event;
- a place/world;
- an echo;
- a live surface;
- a projected future;
- a retired branch.

## Required visible fields

Every tile exposes:

- title
- temporal layer
- status
- date or interval
- place/context
- provenance/source
- confidence/status language appropriate to the object

## Past tile

Must point to an attested/source record.

A reconstruction can add interpretation but cannot overwrite the source.

## Present tile

May be ephemeral until attested.

If recorded, the tile must distinguish:
- observed
- attested
- inferred

## Future tile

Must declare:
- projection status
- starting anchors
- required assumptions
- reachability notes
- design-valley relation
- retirement/falsification conditions

## Forks

A tile may spawn multiple future branches.

Forks are siblings, not competing claims of truth.

## Echoes

An echo references a prior tile/AU while remaining a new present event in its own right.

## Cultural and personal integrity

Source material may be:
- public
- internal
- restricted
- culturally held
- participant-controlled

A public playable rendering must not imply permission to expose underlying restricted source material.
