# Valley / Reachability Model v0

## Thesis

The Playable Future is not "anything someone can narrate."

It is a bounded but potentially enormous set of future states for which a coherent transition path can be described from an attested present.

Let the maintained present state be (S_t).

A projected future (S_f) is **playable** when the projection records a chain of transitions and assumptions connecting (S_t) to (S_f), while clearly distinguishing evidence from inference.

## Constraint families

### Hard / physical
- chronology
- physical law
- material resources
- geography
- technical capability actually available or credibly developed

### Institutional
- law
- contracts
- ownership
- authority
- jurisdiction
- organizational structure

### Social / coordination
- incentives
- number of cooperating actors
- information leakage
- institutional inertia
- culture
- adversarial pressure

### Normative / design
These do not decide what is causally possible. They describe what QuietWire chooses to make easier or harder to reach:
- dignity
- provenance
- consent
- bounded authority
- contestability
- correction
- non-Orwellian visibility of power

## Two valleys, not one

**Reachable valley:** futures for which a coherent causal path exists.

**Design valley:** the subset of reachable futures that QuietWire intentionally tries to deepen through its systems and standards.

A bad future may belong in the reachable valley and still be outside the design valley.

That is useful: the Playable Universe should let people enter failure branches and study how ordinary local decisions created dangerous global outcomes.

## Projection states

A future object should declare one of:

- `illustrative`
- `conditional`
- `reachable_candidate`
- `scenario`
- `retired`
- `contradicted`
- `realized`

None of those means "predicted."

## Reachability burden

A serious future projection should be able to answer:

1. What attested state does it start from?
2. What transitions are required?
3. What assumptions are load-bearing?
4. Which actors must cooperate?
5. What incentives sustain the path?
6. What resources/technology are required?
7. What would falsify or retire the branch?
8. Which rights/authority changes occur?
9. Is the branch merely causally reachable, or also inside the preferred design valley?

## Principle

Constraints do not impoverish the world.

They create meaningful possibility.

The reachable valley can be vast enough for civilizations while still excluding arbitrary or internally incoherent chains of events.
