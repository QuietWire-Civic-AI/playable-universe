# Rights / licensing status

This repository is being bootstrapped while QuietWire develops its Rights OS and legal licensing stack.

## Current rule

Do not infer a new public licence from repository visibility.

The preserved 2025 source snapshots retain the provenance and licensing context of their source repository.

New 2026 documents, schemas, and site code are awaiting a deliberate licensing decision.

## Why this is explicit

The Playable Universe itself is about keeping provenance, interpretation, rendering, and authority distinct.

Its repository should follow the same rule.

A later commit may add:
- a software licence for code/schemas;
- a content licence for documentation/renderings;
- attribution/provenance requirements;
- cultural/participant-specific access constraints where relevant.
