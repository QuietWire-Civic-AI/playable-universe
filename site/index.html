OPEN FIELD TEST

Put one small piece of reality into the universe.

Make a boring claim about something you can actually observe. Tell us how you know it. Add a photograph or coarse location if you want. Playable records the claim and its provenance; it does not declare you correct.

Make an Attest →
  1. 1Say what you attest.“The coffee cup beside me is blue.” is perfect.
  2. 2Say how you know.Direct observation, your own capture, another source, a report, or an inference.
  3. 3Choose what to share.Receipt-only by default; public field stream only when you ask for it.
